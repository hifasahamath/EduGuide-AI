1. G.C.E. ADVANCED LEVEL (A/L) STREAMS & UGC ADMISSION RULES
================================================================================
To be eligible for State University admission in Sri Lanka under the University Grants Commission (UGC):
- Candidates must pass at least 3 subjects in a single G.C.E. A/L attempt.
- Common General Test: Must score a minimum of 30 marks.
- General English: Must sit for the paper (scores used for proficiency ranking).
- Z-Score Mechanism: Standardized Z-Score calculated per subject stream and district quota (40% All-Island Merit, 55% District Merit, 5% Under-privileged Districts).

A/L Streams Overview:
1. Physical Science (Maths Stream): Combined Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry / Higher Maths / ICT.
   -> Pathways: Engineering, Computer Science, Physical Science, Quantity Surveying, Data Science.
2. Biological Science (Bio Stream): Biology, Chemistry, Physics / Agricultural Science.
   -> Pathways: Medicine (MBBS), Dental Surgery (BDS), Veterinary Science, Pharmacy, Agricultural Technology.
3. Commerce Stream: Accounting, Business Statistics / Economics, Business Studies.
   -> Pathways: Management Studies (B.Com, BBA), Financial Mathematics, Accountancy, International Business.
4. Technology Stream: Engineering Technology (ET) or Bio-Systems Technology (BST) + Science for Technology (SFT) + 3rd Subject (ICT, Geography, Economics, Agriculture, etc.).
   -> Pathways: Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech), Engineering Technology, Bio-Process Technology.
5. Arts Stream: Sinhala/Tamil/English Literature, Logic, Political Science, Geography, History, Languages.
   -> Pathways: Bachelor of Arts (BA Hons), Mass Communication, International Relations, Law (LL.B).

3. FINANCIAL AID, SCHOLARSHIPS & LOAN SCHEMES IN SRI LANKA
================================================================================
A. Mahapola Higher Education Scholarship Scheme:
- Administered by: Mahapola Higher Education Scholarship Trust Fund.
- Merit Scholarship: Rs. 5,000 per month (Awarded automatically to high Z-score performers).
- Ordinary Bursary: Rs. 4,500 per month (Awarded based on annual family income below Rs. 500,000).
- Payment Duration: 10 installments per academic year for the full degree duration (3-4 years).

B. Ministry of Education Interest-Free Student Loan Scheme (IFSLS):
- Eligibility: Sri Lankan citizens who passed G.C.E. A/L (in 2023, 2024, or 2025) but missed State University Z-score admission.
- Approved Non-State Institutes: SLIIT, NSBM Green University, IIT, Horizon Campus, CINEC Campus, ICBT, SLTC.
- Financial Coverage:
  * Degree Course Fee: Up to Rs. 800,000 (paid directly to institute).
  * Living Stipend: Rs. 300,000 (Rs. 75,000 per year paid in quarterly installments).
- Repayment Terms: 100% Interest-free during the 4-year study period. Repayment begins 1 year after graduation in equal monthly installments over 7-8 years.

4. POPULAR EXTERNAL & PROFESSIONAL PATHWAYS IN SRI LANKA
================================================================================
1. UCSC External BIT (Bachelor of Information Technology):
   - Minimum Requirement: 3 S passes in A/L (Any stream) OR 6 O/L passes with Credit in Maths & English + Foundation.
   - Flexible online/distance learning degree awarded by University of Colombo.
2. SLATI / ATI (Advanced Technological Institutes):
   - Higher National Diploma (HND) in Engineering, IT, Accountancy (HNDA), English. Free state technical education under Ministry of Higher Education.
3. NVQ Level 6 & 7 (National Vocational Qualification):
   - NVQ Level 5/6: Higher National Diploma level.
   - NVQ Level 7: Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) degree equivalent granted by UNIVOTEC (University of Vocational Technology, Ratmalana).

5. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (Q&A BLOCK)
================================================================================
Q: How do I apply for the Ministry Interest-Free Student Loan Scheme (IFSLS) in Sri Lanka?
A: You can apply online via the Ministry of Education Higher Education Division portal (studentloans.mohe.gov.lk) when the annual call for applications opens. You will need your A/L Index Number, NIC, and preferred non-state degree choice.

Q: Can a student from Commerce or Arts stream study IT or Software Engineering in Sri Lanka?
A: Yes! Programs such as UCSC External BIT, SLIIT BSc in IT, NSBM BSc in Software Engineering, and IIT UK Degree programs accept candidates from ANY G.C.E. A/L stream (Commerce, Arts, Maths, Bio, Technology) provided they meet basic English and Mathematics proficiency.

Q: What is the difference between Mahapola Merit and Mahapola Ordinary scholarship?
A: Mahapola Merit (Rs. 5,000/month) is awarded strictly based on outstanding A/L Z-Score performance, whereas Mahapola Ordinary (Rs. 4,500/month) is awarded based on financial need for students with annual household income under Rs. 500,000.
